## 2. My findings

### Finding 1
- Where: https://mgmt-6110-problem-set-1.vercel.app, on the main screen where TICK shows the task to do
- What I did, what I saw: I opened TICK. It showed "Submit corporate valuation and 10-K financial analysis" as the task to do. The weightage values behind this choice are placeholders, so nothing confirms this task really matters most.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match Between the System and the Real World
- Screen or system: System. Nothing in the data holds real weightage values, so the screen has nothing correct to show.
- Severity, and why: 4, driven by whether it damages the product's standing. The weightages are made up rather than real, so TICK's main recommendation can't be trusted, and it shouldn't be released like this.
- The repair: The task shown first is chosen by its nearest deadline, whether the student has already completed it, and a weightage that matches the official course document.

### Finding 2
- Where: https://mgmt-6110-problem-set-1.vercel.app, main screen, on the deadline of the main task
- What I did, what I saw: I was trying to complete the main task, "Submit corporate valuation and 10-K financial analysis." I tapped the deadline and nothing happened, and nothing on screen pointed me elsewhere. I only found the subtasks by tapping "View action steps and checklist."
- Which heuristic: 4, Consistency and Standards
- Screen or system: Screen. The page already has the checklist and can open it from the deadline.
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by whether it damages trust in the product. When a tap does nothing, the app feels broken.
- The repair: Tapping the deadline opens the action steps and checklist.

### Finding 3
- Where: https://mgmt-6110-problem-set-1.vercel.app, on the bus arrival section
- What I did, what I saw: I looked for a way to choose a different bus stop, one I use more. There's no option, so TICK only shows buses at the one fixed stop. Those may not be the buses that pass the stop I actually use.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match Between the System and the Real World
- Screen or system: System. The back end (api/arrivals.js) only ever fetches stop 04151, so the page can't show any other stop.
- Severity, and why: 3, driven by whether it damages trust in the product. Arrival times for a stop I don't use make the whole recommendation feel irrelevant.
- The repair: I can choose or type my own bus stop, and TICK shows the buses there.
